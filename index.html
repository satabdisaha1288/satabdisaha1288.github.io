About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Biostatistics at MD Anderson Cancer Center. My research focuses on developing Bayesian methods for high-dimensional and compositional data, with applications in microbiome and multi-omics studies. I am particularly interested in variable selection, clustering, and integrating complex biological data to understand disease mechanisms. My work aims to build scalable and principled statistical tools for precision medicine.

Research Interests

  • Bayesian methods for microbiome and multi-omics data
  • Compositional data analysis
  • Variable selection and clustering
  • Graphical models and structured shrinkage
  • Precision medicine and cancer applications
