About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at MD Anderson Cancer Center, where I develop Bayesian statistical methods for high-dimensional and compositional data. My research focuses on microbiome and multi-omics integration, with an emphasis on variable selection, clustering, and predictive modeling. My work is motivated by applications in cancer and aims to advance principled approaches for complex biomedical data.

Research Interests

  • Bayesian methods for microbiome and multi-omics data
  • Compositional data analysis
  • Variable selection and clustering
  • Graphical models and structured shrinkage
  • Biomedical and cancer applications
